## Content Quality & E-E-A-T Findings

### What Works
- Homepage content is exceptional: rich travel narratives, specific data (temperatures, prices), interactive elements, and season-specific descriptions that demonstrate genuine first-hand experience.
- The FAQ page is comprehensive with 12 well-structured questions covering practical traveler concerns.
- Monthly guide page provides granular month-by-month data (temps, rain days, crowd levels, highlights, downsides, packing lists).
- Region guide covers 8 distinct regions with tailored advice for each.
- Content is unique and substantive — no filler, no keyword stuffing, natural language throughout.
- Readability is excellent: varied sentence structure, conversational tone, appropriate for a general travel audience.

### Findings

**High**
- **No author bylines or credentials**: No page identifies who wrote the content. For a YMYL-adjacent topic (travel advice involving safety, costs, logistics), Google expects visible authorship. The "Why Trust This Guide" section on the homepage claims "firsthand experience across 20+ Chinese cities" but doesn't name anyone.
- **Missing "Who / How / Why" signals**: Google's helpful content heuristic asks who created the content, how it was created, and why it exists. None of these answers are visible on any page. The trust section mentions "10-year weather data" and "flight price tracking" but doesn't cite specific sources.
- **No about page**: There is no /about/ page explaining who runs this site, what their qualifications are, or how they gather data. This is the single biggest trust gap.

**Medium**
- **No privacy policy or contact page**: Trustworthiness requires basic legal pages (privacy policy, terms) and a way to contact the site owners. These are absent.
- **Date freshness is hardcoded**: The copyright year and "Updated Jul 2026" text are static. If the site isn't actively maintained, these will become stale quickly, damaging trust.
