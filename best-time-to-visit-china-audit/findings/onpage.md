## On-Page SEO Findings

### What Works
- **Title tags**: All 9 pages have unique, keyword-rich title tags with excellent length (50-70 characters). Homepage title "Best Time to Visit China in 2026: Month-by-Month Guide & Interactive Planner" is particularly strong.
- **Meta descriptions**: All pages have unique, compelling meta descriptions (120-160 characters) with clear value propositions.
- **H1 tags**: Every page has exactly one H1 that aligns with the title tag intent.
- **Heading hierarchy**: Logical H1→H2→H3 structure throughout all pages. No skipped levels.
- **Internal linking**: Excellent cross-linking between pages. Navigation is consistent, footer links all pages, and content sections include relevant CTAs to related content.
- **URL slugs**: Clean, short, descriptive: /when-to-go/, /monthly-guide/, /by-region/, /festivals/, /crowd-calendar/, /faq/, /travel-cost/.

### Findings

**Medium**
- **Homepage H1 is generic**: "Best Time to Visit China" is keyword-rich but could incorporate the year (2026) or a differentiator to better match long-tail queries.
- **No secondary keyword targeting**: While primary keywords are well-covered, subpages don't target secondary/complementary keywords in subheadings. Example: /festivals/ could include headings for "Chinese New Year travel tips" or "Golden Week survival guide" to capture more specific queries.
- **Missing lastmod dates on content pages**: Users and search engines benefit from visible "last updated" dates on content-heavy pages. Only the homepage has a freshness indicator.
