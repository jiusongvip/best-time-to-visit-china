## Search Experience Optimization (SXO) Findings

**Note:** Full SERP analysis requires live Google queries. This assessment is based on content analysis and page-type classification for the target keyword space ("best time to visit China" and related queries).

### Page-Type Match Assessment

The site is structured as a content hub (guide + tools + FAQ). For the primary keyword "best time to visit China", Google SERPs typically reward:
- Comprehensive guide pages (dominant type)
- Month-by-month breakdowns
- Interactive tools (season pickers, quizzes)
- FAQ sections

**Verdict: Strong page-type alignment.** The site's three-route hero ("I know my destination" / "I know my dates" / "I'm starting fresh") maps directly to three traveler intents Google rewards with different result types.

### Findings

**Medium**
- **Homepage tries to be everything**: While the comprehensive approach is valid, Google may prefer more focused landing pages for specific queries (e.g., "best time to visit Beijing in October"). The region-specific pages exist but aren't used as targeted landing pages.
- **Quiz page has static result**: The quiz is interactive but the result logic is client-side only. Google cannot "see" what different quiz paths produce, missing an opportunity for programmatic landing pages for each result combination.

**Low**
- **Consider dedicated landing pages for high-value queries**: "best time to visit Great Wall", "China weather by month", "China travel budget" could each be their own pages rather than sections of the homepage.
